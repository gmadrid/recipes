## Shakshuka
#### stolen from [Downshiftology](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/shakshuka/) but modded by me

Prep time: 10 mins  
Cook time: 20 mins  
Total time: 30 mins  
Serves: 6  

---

### Ingredients

* 2 Tbsp olive oil
* 1 medium onion, diced
* 1 red bell pepper, seeded and diced
* 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
* 2t paprika
* 1t cumin
* 1/4t chili powder
* 1 can (28 oz) whole peeled tomatoes
* 6 large eggs
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* 1 small bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
* 1 small bunch fresh parsley, chopped

---

### Directions

* Heat the oil in a large, deep skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and bell
  pepper and cook ~5 mins, until the onion is soft and translucent.
* Add the garlic, paprika, cumin, and chili powder. Cook ~1 min, until fragrant.
* Pour in the tomatoes with their juice, breaking them up with a spoon. Season with
  salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and let it thicken for a few minutes.
* Make 6 wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one. Season the eggs.
* Cover and cook 5–8 mins, until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny
  (cook longer for firmer yolks). A lid speeds this up.
* Garnish with the cilantro and parsley. Serve with crusty bread or pita.
